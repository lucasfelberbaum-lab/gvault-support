Privacy

Privacy Policy

gVault does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All data — including your vault, coins, badges, and scan history — is stored locally on your device only.

The camera is used solely to scan golf balls in real time. No images or video are ever saved or transmitted.

Notifications are optional and only used for quest reminders. No notification data leaves your device.

gVault contains no third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking of any kind.
